# SECTION 23 — THE SOUL: WRITING & TONE BIBLE [SHIP]

The world loves games with heart before graphics. Writing rules:
23.1 TONE: adventure with warmth and dry humor. Banter, not quips-at-gunpoint.
  Violence has weight and consequence, never glee. The island is beautiful AND
  exploited; the city is dazzling AND cruel. Show both honestly.
23.2 HUMOR: character-driven (Poncho stealing a fish; Hands' terrible
  metaphors; El Sereno offering you tea mid-siege). NEVER punch down. No
  ethnic, gender, disability, or "funny accent" jokes. The setting is a love
  letter to tropical Latin-American/Caribbean rhythms — treat it with respect,
  not costume (checklist in 34.3). No joke that only works in English may sit
  on the critical path (translation-proof humor).
23.3 HEART: Ren's arc is "the man who walks away learns to stand with others."
  Quiet moments are spec'd, not accidental: brother voicemails at campfires,
  Marisol teaching you dominoes, Poncho asleep on your boots during menu idle.
23.4 EL SERENO: one of the great QUIET villains. He never once screeches.
  He offers real philosophy (community vs freedom) so players ARGUE about him
  online for years. Rule: he believes he is the hero of Isla Sombra.
23.5 CONTENT KINDNESS: menu toggle for reduced gore; chapter-head content
  warnings; skip buttons on everything. Respect the player's nervous system.
23.6 ENDINGS (7): both core endings + Stature epilogue variant (26.4) must
  each feel EARNED and discuss-worthy. No "golden ending" that belittles the
  rest. No achievement for suffering (see 29.4).
