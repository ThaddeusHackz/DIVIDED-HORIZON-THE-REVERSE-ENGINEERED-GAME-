# SECTION 26 — THE EMERGENCE ENGINE (a world that gossips about you)

26.1 FACTION WAR SIM: every region has CONTROL % across LA COSECHA / CARTEL
  DEL FARO / MERIDIAN PD / THE PEOPLE. Your actions shift it (loud outpost
  −15% cult; stealth liberation +5% people; police raids bleed cartels). At
  thresholds: guard types flip, shop prices shift (−20% when THE PEOPLE hold),
  street graffiti changes, headlines rewrite (27), and radio editorials turn.
26.2 LOS COBRADORES (nemesis-lite bounty squad) [LOVE]: push Infamy high and
  4 named hunters come for YOU in free roam: "RASTRA" (tracker with a hound —
  Poncho growls before you see her), "VIDENTE" (glass-cannon rooftop sniper),
  "EL PULPO" (netgun brute + 2 goons), "LUCKY" (con-artist who flags you down
  pretending to be a tourist). They remember: wounded hunters return with
  scars and new lines; each falls for good after 3 encounters. Clear the squad
  → unique trinket + Herald obituaries.
26.3 DYNAMIC FREE-ROAM EVENTS [LOVE]: 24 templates, district-weighted,
  cooldown-managed: bank alarm you can join or foil, cartel convoy ambush/
  hijack, street-race signups, a stranded tourist with a suspicious sob story,
  pickpocket chases, a beach rave at night (music swells, dance emote
  encouraged), sudden monsoon stampedes. The island and city are stages, not
  spreadsheets.
26.4 STATURE (Hero ↔ Renegade) [SHIP]: a hidden slider shifted by mercy,
  rescues, thefts, and massacres. It changes crowd greetings (cheer vs
  scatter), radio editorials, prices, which NPCs live to see the finale, and
  the EPILOGUE VARIANT (a third closing cut). Poncho is proud of you either
  way. Never preach — observe.
