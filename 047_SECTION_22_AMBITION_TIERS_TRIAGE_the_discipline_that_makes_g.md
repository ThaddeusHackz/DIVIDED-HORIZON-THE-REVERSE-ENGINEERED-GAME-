# SECTION 22 — AMBITION TIERS & TRIAGE (the discipline that makes greatness possible)

Every Part II feature is tagged:
[SHIP] — must exist at launch. Hard requirement.
[LOVE] — the features players beg their friends to try. Implement after the
  corresponding [SHIP] system is green. Target: ALL of them.
[DREAM] — stretch goals of legend. Build only when [LOVE] is green and tests
  pass. If cut, list honestly in the final report as "next patch".
TRIAGE LAW: Section 18 acceptance is sacred — never trade stability, perf,
  or the .exe for a [DREAM]. A smaller finished game beats a bigger broken one.
GREATNESS LAW: what you ship must be LOVED, not checked off. Every feature
  below ships with juice (Section 32 sound language + game-feel polish) or it
  does not ship at all.
