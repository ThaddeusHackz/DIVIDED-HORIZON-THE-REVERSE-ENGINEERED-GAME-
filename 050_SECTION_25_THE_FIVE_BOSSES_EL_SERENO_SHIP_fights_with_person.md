# SECTION 25 — THE FIVE BOSSES + EL SERENO [SHIP] (fights with personality)

Every boss: intro cutscene-lite (23 rules), pre-fight radio banter, arena with
a 3-phase gimmick, and mid-fight barks reacting to player style (stealth
players hear "WHERE ARE YOU?" search lines; loud players get impressed grunts).
B1 "LA SARGENTO" MARTA QUILES — sugar-mill fortress in the rain. Drill-caller
   who orders shield-wall formations; gimmick: break her cadence by shooting
   the call-drums — silence is the damage phase.
B2 "EL RELOJ" — clocktower bomb-maker. Four bomb-cycles to close blast doors
   on him; gimmick: rotating clock-hand platforms — time itself is the hazard.
   The planned most-clipped fight of the game.
B3 "DOÑA MAREA" — harbor queen. Boat-and-docks pursuit: she rams with an
   armored gunship; gimmick: harpoon fuel lines between passes, then fight
   her on the burning pier. Sunset lighting mandatory.
B4 "EL FRAILE" — silent giant. Bell-tower duel; melee-parry gimmick. Church
   bells audio-telegraph his swings (accessibility by design — 33).
B5 "EL LIMPIADOR" (city finale) — commander of Task Force Limpio. Armored
   downtown assault: SWAT vans reposition as cover; EMP the block to expose him.
FINALE — EL SERENO: lighthouse, monsoon. (i) Calm speech + waves of cultists
   while YOUR liberated garrisons (26.1) hold the flanks — the world you saved
   fights beside you; (ii) he fights — cane blade, precise, polite; (iii) the
   choice (7). Adaptive score (32) drops to near-silence during his speeches.
   Goosebumps are a spec requirement.
